Archive & storage

The archive is separate from transfers. Every captured message is kept locally until you delete it. Transfer settings never delete or trim archived history — large conversations are split into parts instead.

Archive storage …

Deleting archives removes captured conversations, messages and attachment records. It never touches the live conversations on the platforms, and your settings are kept.

Privacy

Local-first by design. The archive lives in this browser's IndexedDB. Relay makes no network requests, has no servers, no analytics and no account. The privacy audit in scripts/lint.js enforces this in CI.

When enabled, Relay also downloads attachments (images/files referenced by messages) from the same site and stores their bytes in the archive. Off by default: without it, only the reference and metadata are kept, and transfers list attachments as “reference only”.

Relay never captures passwords, cookies, hidden system prompts or content outside the conversation. See PRIVACY_POLICY.md for the full data map.
